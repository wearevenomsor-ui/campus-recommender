1. WHY DID I BUILD THIS PROJECT?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
College life is busy. There are hackathons and sports matches and guest speeches 
and musical fests happening every single week. But students miss the best events 
because nobody can keep track of all the announcements.

Right now college notices are messy. Students get blasted with hundreds of spam 
emails and random chat messages. When a student who loves Robotics wants to find 
a workshop they usually miss it because the poster got buried under twenty other flyers.

Adarsh S built this smart app to fix that exact problem. Think of it like Netflix 
or Spotify but built specifically for college students. Instead of showing the 
same boring flyer to everyone it learns what each person likes and shows them 
their own custom feed.


2. HOW DOES THE APP THINK? (THE 2 SMART WAYS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Our engine does not make blind guesses. It combines two smart ways of thinking:

WAY 1: IT LOOKS AT WHAT YOU PERSONALLY LIKE
- This works like a smart match. It checks the topics you typed into your 
  student profile like Python or Robotics or Gaming.
- It reads the title and tags and description of every event on campus.
- If an event teaches Deep Learning with Python the app knows you will love it.
- It works right away even for a brand new student on their first day of college.

WAY 2: IT LOOKS AT WHAT OTHER STUDENTS LIKE
- This works by learning from people with similar taste as you.
- If you and another student both attended the Hackathon the app knows you share 
  similar taste.
- When that student goes to the Robotics Fair and gives it 5 stars the app 
  suggests it to you too.
- It helps you discover exciting new things you might never have thought to search for.

BOTH WAYS COMBINED:
- The app blends both methods (50% personal match and 50% friend picks) into 
  one final score so you get the best of both worlds.


3. SMART FEATURES THAT MAKE THIS APP SUPER SMART
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. WHAT IF A STUDENT JUST JOINED TODAY? (COLD START)
- When a brand new student joins college they have never attended an event yet. 
  A weak app would show a blank page or crash completely.
- Our app switches modes automatically:
  1. If the student typed their interests the app uses 90% of their keywords 
     to find great matches.
  2. If the student has no profile at all the app shows the most popular 
     trending events on campus. It never crashes.

B. GIVING A BOOST TO EVENTS HAPPENING SOON
- Timing is everything in real life. Telling someone about an event that 
  happened yesterday is useless.
- Next 1 to 5 Days: 15% extra boost so you sign up before seats run out.
- Next 2 Weeks: Normal score for advance planning.
- Already Passed: Pushed to the bottom or hidden completely.

C. CLEAR REASONS FOR EVERY SUGGESTION
- Every card on our website shows an exact reason why it was picked:
  * "Matches your interests in Python and Machine Learning"
  * "3 peers with similar taste attended this and gave 5 stars"
  * "Happening soon in the next 5 days"
